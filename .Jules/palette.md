## 2026-10-23 - Interactive HTML Showcase Accessibility
**Learning:** Raw HTML/Tailwind mockup pages in design showcases often miss form field labeling (`aria-label`) and explicit keyboard focus states (`focus-visible:ring-2`), reducing accessibility for screen reader and keyboard-only users.
**Action:** Always ensure input elements have accessible names via `aria-label` or `for` attributes, and add visible focus rings on interactive elements.
